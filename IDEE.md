# IDEE - Temoignages (gameday-temoignages)

Slot d'origine : gameday-p27. Porteur : solo B. Famille : social.
Hackathon : reddit-gameday-2026 / Best Daily Game. Deadline : 09/12/2026 03:00 Paris.

## Pitch (EN)

Four witnesses, one event, who is credible?

## Mechanic

Cross-check testimonies; spot the coherent account.

## Daily loop

One case per day. End card: result, streak, one-tap comment prompt.

## Visual identity

Cassette aesthetic.

## Differentiation

Truth-sifting, comment bait. No overlap with the 29 other submissions nor with r/hotandcold, r/colorpuzzlegame, r/syllo, r/pocketgrids.

## Why it wins

- Delightful UX: distinct art direction, readable in 5 seconds, mobile-first layout.
- Polish: launch-like feel, smooth feedback, zero unstyled components.
- Reddit-y: community scores, streaks and comment hooks; never about Reddit itself.
- Daily Content: deterministic seed shared by all players, fresh every day.

## Risks

- Tonalite sans marque tierce ni personne reelle ; contenus generes inoffensifs.
- Persistance Redis obligatoire (localStorage vide a chaque update).
- Aucune requete externe cote client (CSP) ; sons et fonts embarques.
